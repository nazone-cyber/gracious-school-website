Preparing Leaders and Thinkers

In Grades 4-6, students are developing into independent thinkers and young leaders. Our curriculum deepens academic knowledge across all subjects while emphasizing critical thinking, research skills and real-world application. Students take on greater responsibility for their learning and begin to see how what they learn connects to the wider world.

We maintain the caring community feel that defines Gracious while introducing more challenging academics and leadership opportunities. Students develop confidence, resilience and the skills they'll need to thrive in secondary education.

Character & Values

Beyond academics, Grades 4-6 students are developing strong character, guided by Christian values. We emphasize integrity, responsibility, respect and compassion. Students are encouraged to be good citizens—in school and in their community.

We believe that character matters as much as achievement. Our older students serve as role models for younger students and often take on leadership roles in school activities and celebrations.

Transition to Secondary

By Grade 6, students are ready to transition to secondary education. We ensure they have the academic foundation, study skills and confidence they need to succeed in a larger school environment.

We celebrate Grade 6 students with our annual graduation ceremony—a joyful celebration of their achievements and growth. Many families tell us that starting at Gracious gave their children the confidence and character to thrive in their secondary years.

Celebrating Our Community

Malawi Culture Day: A Living Classroom

Each year, Malawi Culture Day is a highlight of our school calendar. It's a day when our students celebrate the rich diversity, traditions and heritage of Malawi. Students come dressed in traditional attire from different regions and cultures across the country—from Tumbuka to Chichewa, Yao to Ngoni traditions.

Through this celebration, our students learn about their own cultural roots and develop respect and appreciation for the diverse cultures that make up our nation. It's more than a celebration—it's an educational experience that connects learning to real Malawian heritage.

What Students Learn

Understanding and respecting different cultures, connecting with Malawian heritage, celebrating diversity, and developing cultural pride and identity.

Educational Museum Visits

We believe that education happens everywhere—not just in the classroom. Our students regularly take educational trips to the museum, where they can see historical artifacts, learn about Malawian history and culture, and engage with science exhibitions.

These trips bring learning to life. Students interact with exhibits, ask questions and make connections between what they study in class and the wider world around them. It's hands-on, engaging education that sparks curiosity and deeper understanding.

Why Excursions Matter

They provide real-world learning experiences, develop independence and teamwork, spark curiosity about the world, and create lasting memories that strengthen school community bonds.

Annual Graduation: Celebrating Achievement

Every year, we celebrate our graduating Grade 6 students with a memorable graduation ceremony. It's a joyful occasion filled with performances by students, a special meal, professional photoshoots and the traditional mortarboard cap toss.

The graduation ceremony honors the growth and achievements of our students as they complete their primary journey with Gracious. For many families, this is an emotional and joyful milestone—watching their children step forward into their secondary school years with confidence and pride.

Graduation Highlights

Student performances, special dinner celebration, professional photography, cap toss tradition, certificates of achievement, and recognition of each student's unique talents and growth.

Joy in Every Classroom

Beyond these special events, joy and community are woven into every day at Gracious. From spontaneous sing-alongs to birthday celebrations, from group games to collaborative projects—our school is filled with moments of laughter, learning and connection.

We believe that children learn best in an environment where they feel safe, happy and valued. Our teachers create space for play, creativity and genuine relationships. Every child experiences moments of success, friendship and belonging.

Our Culture

Kindness, celebration, discovery, teamwork, mutual respect and a sense of belonging. These values define the Gracious community and shape how our students grow.

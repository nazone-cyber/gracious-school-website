Reception: The Bridge Year

Reception is a special year where children transition from play-based learning to more structured academics. We maintain the warmth and joy of Baby Class while gradually introducing more formal learning. Our approach is developmentally appropriate—children still learn through play and exploration, but with growing emphasis on literacy, numeracy and independence.

In Reception, children develop important social and emotional skills like listening, following instructions, working in groups and taking turns. They build confidence in reading, writing and counting while maintaining their natural curiosity and love of learning.

Reception Curriculum

📖 Early literacyPhonics, letter recognition and beginning to read simple words and sentences
✏️ Early writingLearning letter formation and writing simple words and names
🔢 Numeracy foundationsNumber recognition, counting, basic addition and subtraction through play
🌍 Exploring the worldLearning about our community, nature, seasons and different cultures
🎭 CommunicationSpeaking confidently, listening skills and expressing ideas
⭐ Independence & responsibilitySelf-care, following routines and helping in the classroom

Getting Ready for Grade 1

Reception prepares children for Grade 1 by fostering academic skills alongside emotional and social development. We focus on building their confidence so they feel ready and excited for the next chapter.

By the end of Reception, children can recognize letters and numbers, write their names, understand basic phonics, count to 20 and work cooperatively with peers. Most importantly, they love learning and feel secure in school.

Parent Partnership

We work closely with parents to support each child's development. Regular communication helps us share progress, discuss milestones and address any concerns together.

We encourage families to read with their children at home, practice counting and talking about daily experiences—simple activities that reinforce classroom learning and strengthen the home-school partnership.

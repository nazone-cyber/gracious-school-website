What We Do in Baby Class

Baby Class is all about making school a happy, safe place where little ones can grow. We focus on play-based learning that develops social skills, emotional awareness and early academic foundations. Our warm, caring environment helps each child feel secure and confident as they take their first steps in formal learning.

With a low student-to-teacher ratio, we ensure every child receives the attention and care they need. We celebrate each milestone, from their first friendships to their early attempts at writing and counting.

Key Learning Areas

🎨 Creative playArt, music, and imaginative activities that spark joy and expression
🤝 Social skillsLearning to share, make friends, and interact with care and kindness
📚 Language & literacyStories, singing and early reading readiness through play
🔢 Numbers & patternsCounting, sorting and exploring shapes through hands-on activities
🏃 Physical developmentMovement, coordination and outdoor play to build strength and confidence
❤️ Emotional growthBuilding resilience, managing feelings and Christian values

How We Support Learning

Our Approach

Each day is carefully structured to balance structured learning with free play. We use a mix of teacher-led activities and child-initiated exploration. Our teachers observe each child's interests and abilities, tailoring activities to support their unique development.

We believe that young children learn best through play, so our classroom is filled with engaging materials—blocks, art supplies, music, books and toys that inspire curiosity and creativity.

Class Size & Support

We keep Baby Class groups small, typically 15-18 children per class with one main teacher and a teaching assistant. This ensures each child receives personal attention and care.

We maintain regular communication with parents, sharing updates about your child's progress, achievements and moments of growth. Your partnership in your child's learning is essential to us.

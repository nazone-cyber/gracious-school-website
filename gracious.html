School moments

Celebrating our community.

Explore the vibrant life at Gracious PVT School through our photo galleries of special events, celebrations and everyday learning moments.

Nursery Class Highlights
Our nursery class is full of joy, play-based learning, and precious moments as our youngest learners explore and grow together.
Malawi Culture Day
Each year, our students celebrate Malawi Culture Day, showcasing the rich diversity, traditions and heritage of our nation. Students learn about different cultures and communities across Malawi.
Museum Educational Trip
Learning beyond the classroom! Our students enjoy educational trips to the museum to discover history, science and culture while experiencing hands-on exploration and discovery.
Annual Graduation Celebration
Every year, we celebrate our graduating students with a memorable graduation ceremony featuring performances, a special meal, photoshoots and the traditional cap toss. It's a joyful occasion honoring their growth and achievements.

## How Users Ask About Locations

The website has a **"General Enquiry" form** that allows users to specify what they want to know about. This includes asking about specific locations or information needs.

### Form Options:
- **Admissions** - Questions about enrollment
- **Classes and fees** - School costs and class details
- **School visit** - Requesting a campus tour
- **Transport** - Asking about transportation
- **Other** - Custom questions including location-specific inquiries

## Handling Location-Based Inquiries

### When Parents Ask:
- "Where is your school?"
- "Do you have multiple campuses?"
- "Is there a school closer to my home?"
- "What areas do you serve?"
- "How far is the school from X location?"

1. **Receive Email** - Parent submits enquiry form with location question
2. **Read Inquiry** - Email arrives at nazonenyamutora@gmail.com

## Communication Best Practices

### When Discussing Locations:

✅ **Do:**
- Provide clear, specific directions
- Mention nearby landmarks
- Share travel time from common areas
- Offer to arrange visits
- Provide phone directions support
- Mention any upcoming expansion

❌ **Don't:**
- Make location promises you can't keep
- Ignore transportation concerns
- Dismiss "too far" inquiries
- Forget to follow up
